## Image policy

Do not scrape or commit random Amazon seller images.
Amazon Associates currently provides SiteStripe/Product Links for Amazon product image links, and the operating agreement limits use of Product Advertising Content. The production image should therefore be added through an approved Amazon Associates mechanism or a separately verified/licensed manufacturer asset.
Until that is available, product records intentionally keep image: null and use the site's placeholder artwork.

## Affiliate activation policy
