# SmartBuyIndia Content & SEO Strategy — Phase 3

Status: Planning baseline for the production content system
Date: 8 October 2026
Goal: Build a small, trustworthy Indian product-research site that can earn Amazon Associates commissions without becoming a thin affiliate catalogue.

## 1. Positioning
SmartBuyIndia is an independent Indian product-research and discovery publication.
We do not compete with Amazon on catalogue size. We compete on the work a shopper has to do before opening Amazon: explain the decision, identify the important trade-offs, narrow the field, show why a product fits a use case, explain what could make the shopper choose something else, then send them to Amazon.in to check the live listing.
Every production page must provide meaningful value even if every outbound retailer link is removed.

## 2. Launch model: cluster-first, category-broad
We keep the five top-level categories, but we do not try to make all five equally deep at launch.
Initial publishing focus:
1. Beauty & Grooming — trimmers, shavers and selected hair-care appliances.
2. Office & Study — study/desk lamps, laptop stands and simple desk accessories.
3. Home & Kitchen — electric kettles and other simple, specification-led appliances.
Second wave: Fitness, then broader Electronics & Gadgets.
Amazon's Oct–Nov 2026 fee schedule currently lists Personal Care Appliances at 4.72%, Office Products at 4.25%, Kitchen & Dining at 4%, Sports & Fitness at 4.72%, and Electronics at 2.80%. These rates are planning signals, not guarantees for a specific ASIN.

## 3. Search-intent model
Each production URL gets one primary intent.
A. Decision intent — example: best trimmer for thick beard, electric kettle for office use, study lamp for a small desk.
B. Comparison intent — example: rechargeable vs mains-powered study lamp, 1 L vs 1.8 L kettle.
C. Problem/constraint intent — example: what wattage kettle is practical for an Indian kitchen.
D. Product-detail intent — a product page exists only when we can add useful editorial context around a real product.

## 4. First publishing batch
Target at least 10 original editorial pages before the first serious launch push. Amazon's current application-review guidance gives at least 10 posts as a useful rule of thumb and says sites need robust original content.
Beauty & Grooming:
- Pillar: how to choose a beard trimmer in India.
- Support: trimmer choice for thick/coarse beard.
- Comparison: three researched trimmers for distinct use cases.
Office & Study:
- Pillar: how to choose a study/desk lamp in India.
- Support: rechargeable vs mains-powered study lamp.
- Comparison: three researched study lamps for different desk setups.
Home & Kitchen:
- Pillar: how to choose an electric kettle in India.
- Support: 1 L vs 1.5–1.8 L kettle.
- Support: stainless steel vs glass kettle.
- Comparison: three researched kettles for different household/office needs.
Real product-detail pages are supporting pages attached to these clusters and are published only after research is complete.

## 5. Page relationships
Cluster graph:
Category → pillar guide → supporting guides → comparison → researched product pages.
Back-links:
Product → category + relevant guide + comparison.
Comparison → products + pillar guide.
This gives us useful internal navigation without manufacturing dozens of near-duplicate pages.

## 6. Production article template
Every indexable guide should contain:
1. Search-intent answer in the opening section.
2. Who the guide is for.
3. Buying criteria ordered by importance.
4. India-specific constraints where relevant.
5. A methodology/research note explaining what was checked.
6. Use-case recommendations instead of unexplained global rankings.
7. A comparison table where it clarifies a real choice.
8. Product links only after product data is verified.
9. The case against each recommendation.
10. Genuine FAQ content.
11. Clear affiliate disclosure.
12. Last-updated date and correction route.
Product pages add exact product identity/ASIN, original editorial summary, verified specifications, who it suits, who may want another option, price status, research basis, and related guide/comparison links.
We do not call a page a hands-on review unless we actually used or tested the product.

## 7. Evidence standard
For every real product, research should normally check the Amazon.in product detail page, the manufacturer's official product page or manual, and one additional trustworthy source when independent context is needed.
Record the date checked.
Never copy retailer descriptions, ratings, reviews or product photography as editorial content.
Never publish a claim simply because many other affiliate pages repeat it.

## 8. Claims we avoid
Unless genuinely supported by our own evidence, do not use: #1/best overall, Amazon's best, guaranteed results, clinically proven, tested/reviewed by us, lowest price, lowest ever, massive discount, limited stock, review scores, aggregate ratings, or invented specifications.
Use a use-case label plus a reason instead.

## 9. Price and deal policy
A price is published only when a person has checked the live Amazon.in listing and recorded the date.
A deal is published only when the original price, current price, date and applicable terms are recorded. The discount percentage is calculated from observed values.
Expired or stale offers are removed rather than left online.

## 10. SEO architecture
Production pages use clean browser URLs, one descriptive H1, unique title and meta description, canonical URLs, breadcrumbs, internal links, appropriate structured data, and a production-only sitemap.
Search pages and other utility pages remain noindex.
We will not add Product/Review/AggregateRating structured data merely to chase rich results. Structured data must match the actual page and verified data.

## 11. Publication states
Practical states: research → draft → reviewed → published.
Only published, non-demo records are eligible for production navigation, indexing and sitemap inclusion.

## 12. Launch gate
Before a page becomes published:
- content is original;
- primary search intent is clear;
- facts are checked;
- product identity/ASIN is verified where applicable;
- Amazon target link is valid;
- price is dated if shown;
- unsupported ratings/reviews are absent;
- affiliate disclosure is present;
- title/meta/H1 match the page;
- internal links connect the cluster;
- correction/report route exists;
- the editor can explain why the page is worth visiting instead of going directly to Amazon.

## 13. Deliberately not doing yet
No mass product import.
No automatic Amazon catalogue scraping.
No AI-generated page farm.
No large generic best-X list for every category.
No deal page padded with unverified offers.
No Google indexing of demo content.
No paid SEO traffic yet.
No broad social-content production before the first useful articles exist.

## 14. Measurement loop
For each cluster, track Search impressions, clicks, queries, organic landing pages, outbound Amazon clicks, click-through rate, qualifying purchases, revenue per page, and pages with impressions but weak clicks or clicks but weak conversion.
The publishing queue changes from evidence, not from a rigid calendar.

## 15. Current external guidance
Amazon India Associates currently requires clear disclosure and identification as an Amazon Associate, prohibits inaccurate or misleading product claims, and says the site should provide robust original content for application review.
Google currently emphasizes people-first content, original information/analysis, useful depth, clear expertise/background and substantial value beyond what already exists elsewhere.
These sources should be re-checked before public launch because program rules and search guidance can change.
